# Aaron Yu — Personal Website Plan

## Content

- **Introduction:** Aaron Yu, a 5th Form student in class 5B at Auckland Grammar School.
- **What I care about:** Music, gaming with friends, and learning languages.
- **Now:** Saxophone performances with Concert Band, Symphony Orchestra, and Big Band; French study at B1 level.
- **Interests:** Minecraft, Brawl Stars, and other games played with friends.
- **Opportunities:** A curated list of things Aaron follows and may apply for, such as programmes, competitions, events, or roles.
- **Weekly:** A newsletter archive or signup area for weekly writing and updates.

## Section order

1. **Hero** — Name, short student introduction, and a clear current focus.
2. **About** — School year and class, with a short personal snapshot.
3. **What I care about** — Music, friendships through games, and French learning.
4. **Working on now** — Saxophone ensembles and B1 French study.
5. **Opportunities** — Saved opportunities Aaron is tracking.
6. **Weekly** — Newsletter introduction and latest issues.
7. **Footer** — Simple contact or social links when available.

## Visual direction

- Use a clean, personal editorial layout with ample space and a strong type hierarchy.
- Let the site feel curious and youthful, with restrained colour accents that can nod to music and games without becoming playful or cluttered.
- Make the hero immediately readable, then use short cards or list rows for current work, opportunities, and newsletter posts.
- Keep the experience mobile-friendly, with simple navigation and obvious paths into Opportunities and Weekly.
- A design reference was not present in the project folder, so its visual specifics should be incorporated when it is supplied.

## What we build today

- A static personal website driven by a local content file.
- The core pages or sections: Home, Opportunities, and Weekly.
- Reusable presentation for Aaron's profile, current work, tracked opportunities, and newsletter entries.
- No agent functionality, automation, or external data connection; those belong to the later lesson.
